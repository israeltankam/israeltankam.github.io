Model & concepts

The object model and continuous-time Markov chain used by Cropmix.

Object hierarchy#

The design deliberately separates concerns so that the same epidemic model can be reused across fields and crops.

LayerMain objectResponsibility
GeometryFieldFinite set of two-dimensional planting coordinates.
PlantingMixtureDesignExactly one cultivar label per field coordinate.
Host biologyVarietyTransmission, latency/roguing and healthy/infected yield.
Vector/pathogenVectorParameters, PathogenParametersVector mortality/movement and loss of infectivity / transmission mode.
MovementExponentialKernelDistance-weighted, symmetrically balanced non-local movement.
SystemCropMixSystemCombines varieties, vector biology, pathogen biology and movement.
ExperimentScenarioDuration, vectors per plant and initial plant/vector infection pressure.
OutputSimulationResultRun-level stochastic trajectories, endpoint outcomes, maps and metadata.

Spatial CTMC#

For site i, the plant state is S (susceptible/uninfected), L (infected but not yet infectious) or I (infectious). The local vector population is partitioned into virus-free vectors and virus-bearing provenance classes. The key local invariant is:

Hi + Σk Vi(k) = m

The principal state-changing events are inoculation, end of plant latency, optional roguing, acquisition, vector clearance, vector mortality/replacement and reciprocal vector exchange.

Plant infection#

A susceptible plant at site i becomes latent at a rate determined by its cultivar-specific inoculation parameter and the local number of virus-bearing vectors.

Vector acquisition#

Virus-free vectors on an infectious plant acquire infection at the cultivar-specific acquisition rate; their provenance class records the cultivar on which acquisition occurred.

Plant progression#

Latent plants progress to infectiousness at the cultivar-specific latent progression rate. Optional roguing returns infectious plants according to the specified roguing rate.

Vector loss#

Virus-bearing vectors can lose infectivity through clearance and through mortality/replacement, returning to the virus-free class.

What is and is not inferred#

Cropmix is a simulation and design framework. It does not automatically infer every biological parameter from spatial field outcomes. Acquisition, inoculation and clearance can be informed through EpiPvr access-period experiments; plant latency, yields and movement scale require their own evidence or experimental design.

Transmission-mode limitThe current spatial engine implements semi-persistent transmission (SPT). Persistent-transmission parameterisations that require an exposed/latent vector compartment are rejected rather than silently coerced into the SPT state space.

Mean-field, branching process and spatial CTMC answer different questions#

Cropmix keeps these modelling layers separate because they answer different questions. EpiPvr branching-process calculations concern establishment near invasion; the deterministic mean-field system is a well-mixed population reference; the spatial CTMC retains finite numbers, explicit coordinates, planting arrangement and stochasticity.
