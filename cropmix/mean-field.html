Mean-field models

Solve the deterministic reference model and assess when well-mixed dynamics approximate spatial simulations.

Deterministic mean-field solver#

solve_mean_field
solve_mean_field(design, system, scenario, *, observation_times=None)

Solve the generalized n-variety PLOS-style SPT reference with SciPy LSODA. Observation times default to 101 points. PT is not supported by this solver.

Mean-field adequacy diagnostic#

assess_mean_field_consistency
assess_mean_field_consistency(field, system, scenarios, *, reference_variety, scales=None, n_runs=50, observation_times=None, incidence_weight=0.5, vector_weight=0.5, bootstrap_reps=100, seed=20260810)

Run a monoculture reference across multiple scenarios and movement scales, comparing mean spatial incidence/vector trajectories against mean field. Default scales are 100 log-spaced values from 0.05 to 100.

The discrepancy is a weighted average of incidence RMSE and vector-prevalence RMSE. Final yield error is recorded as an external diagnostic but is not part of the score. The function reports a literal score-minimising scale, minimax scale, one-SE acceptable interval, a practical one-SE selected threshold and leave-one-context-out sensitivity.

Legacy alias#

calibrate_kernel
calibrate_kernel(*args, **kwargs)

Deprecated alias that emits DeprecationWarning and forwards to assess_mean_field_consistency(). KernelCalibrationResult is also an alias of MeanFieldConsistencyResult.

Scientific interpretationThe selected scale is a threshold for practical mean-field adequacy in the tested contexts, not a biological estimate of insect movement. Use independent literature/tracking/spatial evidence for the biological kernel scale.
