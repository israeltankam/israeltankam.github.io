Model boundaries

Current scientific and computational scope of Cropmix 0.2.0.

Transmission model#

The spatial simulator and deterministic mean-field solver implement semi-persistent transmission (SPT). PathogenParameters and the EpiPvr bridge can represent persistent-transmission (PT) parameter inference, but a PT CropMixSystem is rejected by the spatial simulator because PT dynamics require an explicit vector latent compartment.

Field size and movement matrices#

The geometry-general simulator supports arbitrary finite 2-D coordinates, but its movement preparation is dense and therefore scales quadratically in memory with the number of planting sites. The specialised cropmix.publication_backend avoids that dense matrix for large binary square lattices, at the cost of stricter geometry and variety assumptions.

Spatial metrics#

pairwise_mixing_metrics() is defined on arbitrary coordinates through an explicit distance radius. The 12 additional arrangement metrics in cropmix.publication_backend operate on binary square grids. Their definitions should not be applied to arbitrary geometry or multi-variety designs without an explicit extension.

Optimisation#

optimize_mixture() uses count-preserving simulated annealing. It is a heuristic search: the best returned design is the best design found under the declared objective, seed, Monte Carlo effort and annealing schedule, not a proof of a global optimum.

Biological parameterisation#

Cropmix consumes biological parameters; it does not infer every parameter from spatial epidemic data. Transmission parameters can be supplied directly or derived from access-period experiments through the optional EpiPvr bridge. Movement scale, plant progression, roguing and yield parameters require independent evidence appropriate to the target system.

Vector burden#

Scenario.vectors_per_plant is an integer model quantity. Mapping field vector observations to this quantity is part of study design and validation; the package does not automatically convert trap counts, visual counts or density indices into vectors per plant.

Yield model#

Each Variety supplies healthy and infected yield values in a user-chosen common unit. Cropmix 0.2.0 does not include mechanistic crop growth, infection-time-dependent yield loss, market prices or resource competition unless those are incorporated externally or through a custom optimisation objective.

Parameter uncertainty#

A simulation can receive one coherent TransmissionDraw. Full posterior predictive propagation is performed by an outer workflow that samples joint parameter draws and calls the simulator repeatedly; Cropmix does not silently resample marginal posterior distributions inside simulate_mixture().

Validation#

Agreement between spatial and mean-field models is a model-consistency diagnostic, not empirical validation. Predictive validation requires independent biological parameterisation and spatial field observations.
