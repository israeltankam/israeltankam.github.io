Reproducible aggregate dataset

nemo-pcn/data/published_greenhouse_summary.csv contains four positive inoculation levels for trial 1 and pooled trials 2/3, with the published mean cyst density, mean eggs per cyst, final egg density and reported standard errors. It does not contain fabricated pot-level observations. The zero-inoculation control is not included in the positive-density recruitment fit because its biological role differs from the nonlinear recruitment observations.

Data permissions and journal licensing apply to reproduced published values. When sharing an expanded dataset, confirm the primary paper and any applicable use conditions.
