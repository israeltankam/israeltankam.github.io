Virulence, drift and reproductive bottlenecks

Selected and neutral frequencies

The virulence allele is selected on resistant hosts because only aa juveniles can recruit reproductive females. A neutral genetic marker, in contrast, has no direct effect on its carrier's sex or establishment probability. The neutral-marker module assumes free recombination between loci.

result = nemo.simulate_neutral_marker(
    parameters=nemo.Parameters(b=0),
    schedule=nemo.Schedule('R',years=20),
    initial_density=20,virulence_frequency=.2,
    marker_frequency=.4,area_m2=.0054,
    n_replicates=2000,seed=42)
print(result.summary())

Frequencies are reported among surviving populations. The survival fraction is always retained, because excluding extinct replicates changes the conditioning of any frequency estimate.

Sex-ratio effective-size proxy

The quantity $N_e^{sex}=4MF/(M+F)$ summarises the effect of unequal reproductive sex numbers. It is not a generally exact genetic effective population size: variance of family size, consanguinity, selection and spatial mating structure may further alter true drift rates.

Initial reproductive failure

The following expression is exact under the stated independent initial genotype assignment and establishment assumptions:

risk = nemo.female_recruitment_risk(
    q0=.01,area_m2=.0054,initial_density=80)
print(risk)

The expected number of virulent females depends on both absolute population size and $q_0^2$. A small focal population can fail to recruit any females on the first resistant crop, even if the corresponding deterministic fraction remains positive.

Contrasting sources of variability

experiments = nemo.compare_stochasticity_mechanisms(
    schedule=nemo.Schedule('R',years=20),
    area_m2=.0054,virulence_frequency=.2,n_replicates=2000)
summary = nemo.summarize_stochasticity_comparison(experiments)

The partial-stochasticity models are explicit counterfactual numerical diagnostics. Their results depend on intervention definitions and integer rounding; they should not be described as a unique or exact causal decomposition of multi-generation variability. For the conditional allele-copy variance, selected_allele_variance_components() reports the algebraic separation between offspring-number randomness and genetic combination randomness, given the breeders.

Fixation and evolutionary rescue

print(nemo.fixation_window(nemo.Parameters(),r=4))

Fixation is not synonymous with evolutionary rescue. Demonstrating sustained rescue requires distinguishing fixation preceding extinction from fixation followed by long-term persistence, especially when the population has very few breeders.
