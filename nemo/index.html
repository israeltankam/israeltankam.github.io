NEMO-PCN

Population dynamics, virulence evolution, and management of potato cyst nematodes.

**NEMO-PCN** links measurable infestation and virulence with deterministic and individual-based stochastic population predictions. The core model is an explicit yearly life cycle, with susceptible, resistant and non-host crop years and time-varying suicide-hatching control.

Main capabilities

• Diploid one-locus genotype tracking and genotype-specific reproductive contributions

• Binomial establishment and sex determination, Poisson reproduction and egg survival

• Numba-accelerated independent Monte Carlo simulations

• Estimation using density, cyst recruitment, fecundity, count, virulence and hatching data

• Exportable density trajectories, allele frequencies and extinction probabilities

• Repeating crop schedules and annual control efficacies

import nemo_pcn as nemo
result = nemo.simulate(nemo.Scenario(cycle="RNN", years=30), n_runs=1000)
print(result.summary())

This package models *G. pallida* with a specified genetic architecture. Predictions require field calibration, and the absence of detected nematodes in a sampled soil volume is not identical to verified field eradication.
