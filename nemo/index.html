NEMO-PCN

Stochastic demo-genetic modelling and integrated management of the potato cyst nematode.

NEMO-PCN is a scientific Python library for the simulation, calibration and quantitative comparison of population management programmes targeting Globodera pallida. It follows individual offspring counts by genotype, within annual programmes of susceptible or resistant potato cultivation, nonhost rotations and suicide-hatching biocontrol.

The modelling components are intended to work together without becoming inseparable: demographic observations are fitted under a declared biological model, stochastic and deterministic trajectories are evaluated under identical crop programmes, and management outcomes are summarised at explicit planning horizons.

Principal operations

import nemo_pcn as nemo

result = nemo.run_simulation(
    nemo.Parameters(),
    nemo.Schedule('RNN', years=30),
    virulence_frequency=0.2,
    initial_density=20,
    area_m2=0.05,
    n_replicates=1000, seed=42)
print(result.outcomes((5,10,20,30)))
  • Simulate: stochastic population trajectories and a deterministic reference.
  • Estimate: demographic and genetic parameters from observation-specific data.
  • Compare: susceptible cultivation, masculinizing resistance, host-free rotation and suicide-hatching efficacy.
  • Explore drift: selected virulence, a separate neutral marker, the reproductive bottleneck and demographic-noise diagnostics.
  • Export: reproducible data tables, scenario metadata and publication-quality graphs.

Scientific foundations

The life cycle and genetic response are based on the model of Tankam Chedjou et al. (2024), Evolutionary Applications. The greenhouse aggregate example is based on Contina, Dandurand and Knudsen (2019), Plant Disease. Results are conditional predictions, not field-validated quarantine clearance probabilities.
