Calibration

Cysts and eggs per cyst

fit_two_stage() fits establishment, competition and fecundity to paired inoculation, cyst and fecundity observations, with optional errors of published means. The data should be provided in columns Pi_eggs_per_g, cysts_per_g, eggs_per_cyst, cysts_se, eggs_se.

Initial–final density

fit_density() uses weighted least squares on Pi_eggs_per_g, Pf_eggs_per_g, optionally Pf_se, and a specified competing regulation hypothesis.

Count data

fit_counts() provides Poisson or negative-binomial likelihoods for *actual nonnegative replicate counts* and a user-supplied mean function. An aggregated mean is not a count.

Molecular frequency and hatching data

fit_virulence() handles virulent_copies and total_copies. fit_hatching() handles hatched and total_eggs. qPCR calibration with Ct values or field-specific diagnostic sensitivity requires an explicit observation model rather than pretending Ct values are allele counts.

For every fitted model, inspect structural identifiability, parameter boundaries, residuals, independent trials and uncertainty transfer to management outcomes.
