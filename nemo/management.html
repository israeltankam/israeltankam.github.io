Rotations and suicide-hatching biocontrol

Repeating crop sequences

A schedule is a sequence of calendar years. For example, RN means one resistant potato year followed by one nonhost year; RNN means one resistant year in three. RSN introduces a susceptible potato year between resistant cultivation and the nonhost period. The number of potato crops is an explicit consequence of the schedule rather than an implicitly assumed rotation index.

for cycle in ('R','RN','RNN','RNNN','RNNNN','RSN','SRN'):
    print(cycle, nemo.Schedule(cycle,years=12).crops)

Annual biocontrol efficacy

b represents additional loss in the current year due to a treatment such as suicide hatching. It is distinct from the physical proportion of eggs that hatch and distinct from an efficacy observed after one field application. High values should be interpreted as scenarios unless independently validated for the proposed repeated programme.

constant = nemo.Schedule('RNN', years=30, biocontrol=.45)
annual = nemo.Schedule('RNN', years=6,
                       biocontrol=[.20,.70,.85,.20,.60,.75])

Comparing strategies

results = nemo.compare_strategies(
    cycles=['RN','RNN','RNNN','RNNNN','RSN','SRN'],
    biocontrol=[0,.2,.4,.6,.8,.9],
    virulence=[.05,.2,.6],
    years=50,horizons=[5,10,20,30,40,50],
    n_replicates=1000,area_m2=.05)

The returned table reports estimated probability of being below threshold, sustained suppression, eradication, number of potato crops and mean density. It permits transparent comparison of rapidity and agricultural exposure; it does not estimate economic costs without additional data.

Management endpoints

  • Below threshold: density at a specific horizon is below the chosen agronomic threshold.
  • Ever below threshold: the threshold was crossed at least once before the horizon.
  • Sustained suppression: a full window of consecutive years was observed under the threshold.
  • Eradication: the integer population reached the absorbing state zero in the closed system.

Official quarantine release and post-treatment sampling require procedures outside this model.
