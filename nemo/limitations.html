Assumptions, diagnostics and responsible interpretation

Demographic assumptions

The current model has one generation per potato crop, does not resolve within-year root ecology, treats all eggs as hatching on host encounter, and uses constant demographic parameters unless the calendar-specific biocontrol rate is supplied. Field data may support more detailed stage-dependent mortality and recruitment than can be identified from egg densities alone.

Genetic assumptions

The selected locus is diallelic and virulence recessive. Sex-dependent selection on masculinizing resistance is represented; mutation, multiple resistance loci, migration, explicit relatedness and linkage are not part of the standard three-genotype engine. Neutral-marker analysis represents one unlinked locus and cannot by itself recover the full effective population size or the mating neighbourhood in a field.

Spatial assumptions

Area controls the number of simulated nematodes, not a spatial movement process. Panmixia across a whole mapped infestation may overestimate the effective number of breeders. The isolated-patch diagnostic is a sensitivity exercise, not a diffusion model.

Calibration limits

Different experimental designs can identify different demographic components. Mean cyst recruitment and mean eggs per cyst should not be reduced to a single Pf statistic if both are observed. Fits to summary means are conditional on the reported standard errors and need independent evaluation. Observations from a different population or soil should not be pooled automatically.

Quarantine and agronomy

Globodera pallida is a regulated quarantine pest. Output probabilities should assist scenario prioritisation, sampling design and protocol assessment, not replace official surveillance or statutory restrictions. Returning below one egg per gram in simulation is not evidence that all cysts have disappeared from the field.

Technical scope

The Python stochastic kernel is accelerated by Numba. It supports integer populations that fit in signed 64-bit counts. Very large area and large initial densities may exceed useful computational resources. For extremely large populations a deterministic approximation may be appropriate, with local stochastic modelling where extinction or rare-genotype events matter.
