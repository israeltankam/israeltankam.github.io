Scientific model

The state at a crop-year boundary is $(N_{AA},N_{Aa},N_{aa})$. The virulence allele frequency and virulent homozygote frequency are distinct: $q=(N_{Aa}+2N_{aa})/(2N)$ and $v=N_{aa}/N$. When Hardy–Weinberg is assumed at initialization, the genotype vector is $((1-q)^2,2q(1-q),q^2)$.

Annual processes are establishment, sex determination, fertilization and survival. In resistant potato crops, avirulent juveniles (AA and Aa) develop as males. The paternal genotype distribution follows the realized male pool. There is no mating if either sex has zero breeders.

The establishment-regulation candidate uses $s_{eff}=s/(1+\alpha n)$; the original fecundity-regulation candidate uses $e_{eff}=e/(1+cF/\kappa)$. These hypotheses should be calibrated separately.

Egg survival includes background mortality, spontaneous hatching and an additional mortality probability $b_t$ corresponding to the biocontrol schedule. A treatment-induced hatching percentage is not automatically equal to net viable-egg removal.

The spatial extent determines absolute counts under a local panmixia assumption. It is not a dispersal model.
