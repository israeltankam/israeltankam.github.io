Simulation and management

from nemo_pcn import Scenario, Parameters, simulate, compare_strategies

scenario = Scenario(cycle="RSN", years=40, area_m2=.1,
                    initial_virulence=.08, initial_density=15,
                    biocontrol_by_year=tuple([.2,.7,.7]*13+[.2]))
r = simulate(scenario, Parameters(), n_runs=5000, seed=2026)
r.quantiles().to_csv("trajectory_quantiles.csv", index=False)
r.summary(horizons=(5,10,20,30,40)).to_csv("outcomes.csv", index=False)

The summary method reports the probability of being below a density threshold at a given year separately from complete extinction. Frequencies after extinction remain undefined. Comparisons between S, R, N, RN, RNN, RSN and SRN should be made on common calendar horizons. Median trajectories must not be confused with an individual trajectory.
