Quick start

Step 1: describe the biology

import nemo_pcn as nemo

biology = nemo.Parameters(
    e=398.5188, s=0.25, alpha=0.1438,
    mu=0.10, h=0.17, m=0.35, b=0.45,
    regulation='establishment')

Here e denotes eggs per female, s the establishment probability, m the male probability on a susceptible cultivar, mu yearly natural egg mortality, h spontaneous hatching without host and b an additional yearly biocontrol loss probability. The default parameterisation is a working example, not a universal field estimate.

Step 2: choose a management programme

S denotes susceptible potato; R denotes masculinizing-resistant potato; N denotes a nonhost year. 'RNN' produces a resistant crop every three calendar years.

calendar = nemo.Schedule('RNN', years=30)
print(calendar.table(biology.b).head())

Step 3: simulate

result = nemo.run_simulation(
    biology, calendar,
    initial_density=20,
    virulence_frequency=.20,
    area_m2=.05,
    n_replicates=1000, seed=21)

Step 4: inspect annual density and allele frequencies

print(result.summary('density'))
print(result.summary('allele'))
print(result.summary('homozygote'))

Frequencies are undefined after extinction and stored as missing values. Density is zero after extinction. Shaded trajectory intervals are prediction intervals across independent replicate populations, not uncertainty intervals around their mean.

Step 5: evaluate agricultural endpoints

print(result.outcomes((5,10,20,30)))

The table distinguishes the probability of ending below the chosen density threshold, of remaining below it for the specified verification window, and of reaching the absorbing extinction state.

Step 6: save an analysis

from nemo_pcn.viz import plot_trajectories, save_figure
result.export('outputs/demo',raw=False)
fig = plot_trajectories(result)
save_figure(fig,'outputs/demo/density.pdf')
